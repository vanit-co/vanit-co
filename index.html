Lichen

One platform beneath your operations

Lichen sits between your devices and your business systems. It keeps the connections, the history, the alarms and the automation running, and answers every system in front of it through one API.

Connect any site

Read PLCs, meters and brokers over the protocols the plant already speaks, from the server or from an edge gateway at the site.

  • Modbus TCP and RTU, OPC UA, EtherNet/IP, Siemens S7, MQTT with Sparkplug B, BACnet/IP, DNP3, SNMP and HTTP APIs
  • Edge gateways installed with one line, upgraded remotely, rolled back on failure
  • Every link measured: uptime, response time and failed reads

History you can trust

Every point is kept as it changes, with its quality, and queried by the minute or by day, week and month in your time zone.

  • Sampling and recording set apart, with deadband and heartbeat
  • Time-weighted averages, counters, run hours and starts
  • Live values and trends for every asset

Alarms that stand up to an audit

Alarms are decided where the point is read, even with no server in reach, and measured the way ISA-18.2 measures them.

  • Thresholds with hysteresis and debounce, or a rule over several points
  • Acknowledgement, shelving, and escalation to your paging system
  • A journal of floods, bad actors, chattering and time to acknowledge

See failures coming

Lichen ranks every watched point by how soon its trend reaches the limit, so the maintenance visit is planned before the failure.

  • The last fortnight's trend held against each alarm limit
  • A point against its own baseline, this week against last
  • Hour by hour, day by day, to find when a problem starts

Automate what the site needs done

Jobs start on a schedule, on a changing value, on an alarm or on request, and every run is kept.

  • Calls to other systems, scripts and point writes, retried with backoff
  • Commands written at most once and confirmed by reading the device back
  • Scripts and services in Python or TypeScript, supervised by Lichen
